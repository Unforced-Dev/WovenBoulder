Meeting Overview

The Board of Zoning Adjustment held a regular meeting to consider a setback variance request for a residential property at 2305 Forest Avenue. The applicant sought approval to construct a new second-story addition with an enlarged partially covered rooftop deck that would encroach within the required 12.5-foot side-adjacent-to-street setback. The board reviewed staff presentation, heard from the applicant, considered neighbor feedback, and voted on the variance request.

Key Items

Setback Variance for 2305 Forest Avenue (Docket No. DZ-2022-0)

  • Request for variance to minimum side adjacent-to-street setback standards for a principal structure in the RL-1 zoning district
  • Proposal: New second-story addition with partially covered rooftop deck above existing garage
  • Current setback: Existing garage sits at approximately 7.6 feet (non-conforming)
  • Required setback: 12.5 feet per code
  • Proposed deck railings: Approximately 7.6 feet from west property line
  • Deck extension: Approximately 4-5 feet beyond the by-right design already approved under building permit
  • Neighbor support: Four written letters of support received from neighboring properties
  • Staff recommendation: Support the variance as a minor modification that optimizes usable space without impacting bulk, sideyard articulation, or visual character

Administrative Items

  • December meeting minutes deferred to next month's approval
  • Board soliciting volunteers for upcoming Board of Zoning Adjustment vacancies; deadline January 30
  • ADU standards presentation scheduled for February 14 meeting

Outcomes and Follow-Up

  1. Motion to approve Docket No. DZ-2022-0 (setback variance for 2305 Forest Avenue) passed unanimously by all board members
  2. Applicant must return to building department for revised building permit incorporating the enlarged deck design
  3. ADU standards presentation (with 80-use evaluation and historical context) to be provided to board members one week before February 14 meeting
  4. Board members requested to confirm calendar availability for February 14 ADU presentation meeting
  5. Staff to provide information to interested volunteers for Board of Zoning Adjustment vacancies by January 30 deadline
  6. December meeting minutes deferred to next meeting agenda
