Meeting Overview

This was an All Commissions Summit meeting bringing together representatives from multiple Boulder municipal advisory bodies including the Downtown Management Commission, BJAD Parking Commission, University Hill Commercial Area Management Commission, and others. Staff presented updates on the city's economic vitality strategy, commercial district initiatives, and upcoming projects including the Spruce Garage construction and Downtown Development Authority (DDA) exploration.

Key Items

Staff Introductions and Commissioner Remarks

  • Mark Wolf, Regan Brown (Senior Project Manager, Community Vitality), Christine Edwards, and other city staff introduced themselves
  • Commissioners from across districts shared accomplishments including the recent Roots Music Festival, outdoor dining pilot program, and the Ambassador Program for non-police community support
  • Multiple commissioners expressed pride in the two new hotels on the Hill and connections being built between downtown and the University Hill area

Economic Vitality Strategy Update

  • Updated economic vitality strategy approved by City Council in May 2025 — first update since 2013
  • Strategy organizes around four focus areas: equity, resilience, vitality, and innovation
  • Council established two key priorities: updating the economic vitality strategy and enhancing programs to address commercial vacancies and support small businesses
  • Discussion included the Sundance resort coming to Boulder and the need to connect the Hill and downtown commercial areas as one experience

Spruce Garage Construction

  • Construction on the Spruce Garage expected to start as early as late January or early February
  • Approximately 20% of people visiting downtown park in these garages, making their maintenance and expansion critical

Downtown Development Authority (DDA) Project

  • Work ongoing to explore establishing a DDA
  • A plan of development to be produced with input from commissioners and the business community to establish priorities
  • This document will be essential before the proposal reaches the ballot

Cities Work Partnership

  • City partnering with Cities Work, a nonprofit providing free technical assistance to analyze small business experience with city processes and identify barriers to entry

Commercial District Initiatives

  • Downtown business newsletter established 6-8 weeks ago
  • Affordable commercial pilot grants distributed
  • Broadway median improvements underway in the Hill area
  • Communication and maintenance improvements continuing in limited funding environment

Economic Development Programs

  • Council approved an expanded economic incentive program allowing the city to waive certain fees for businesses expanding or relocating
  • Ordinance approved earlier in the month

Urban Renewal and CHIP Zones

  • Boulder Urban Renewal Authority (BURA) has been inactive for 10 years; city exploring potential use in Boulder Junction, East Bookend, and Civic areas
  • City Council established 3 CHIP zones to support advanced manufacturing businesses; state application pending for November approval
  • Metro districts being considered for targeted cases

RTD Bus Service

  • Commissioners noted gratitude for restored bus service to the Boulder Junction area, which had been suspended

Outcomes and Follow-Up

  1. DDA plan of development to be developed with stakeholder input over coming months to establish priorities and prepare for potential ballot measure
  2. Spruce Garage construction to commence late January/early February
  3. Cities Work partnership launched to reduce barriers for small business city processes
  4. Economic incentive program ordinance approved; formal program implementation underway
  5. BURA reactivation under consideration with potential application to Boulder Junction, East Bookend, and Civic areas
  6. CHIP zone applications to state pending, targeting November approval
  7. Continued collaboration among commissions and city staff on economic development initiatives, particularly connecting Hill and downtown areas
  8. Broadway median improvements continuing
  9. Downtown business newsletter to continue as ongoing communication tool
