About this site

Boulder Civics indexes meetings from all of Boulder's governing bodies — City Council, Planning Board, Housing Advisory Board, Human Relations Commission, Transportation Advisory Board, Police Oversight Panel, Landmarks Board, Parks and Recreation Advisory Board, and more — with YouTube recordings and AI-generated summaries where available.

Use the search bar above to find discussions on any topic: housing, transportation, equity, climate, surveillance, the budget, and more. Or browse the timeline to see how issues evolved across bodies over time.

Meeting records sourced from City of Boulder YouTube. This is an independent civic resource, not affiliated with the City of Boulder.
