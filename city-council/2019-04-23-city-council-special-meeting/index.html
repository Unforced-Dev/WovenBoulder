Date: April 23, 2019 Type: Special Meeting

Meeting Overview

Special meeting featuring presentations on permanent supportive housing outcomes, family homelessness prevention, and Census 2020 preparations. The council saw exceptional performance data from a 31-unit supportive housing facility at Lee Hill Road and outcomes from the two-year Keep Families Housed rental assistance pilot. The ISLA (Latin American student) program at Boulder High School was also proclaimed.

Key Items

ISLA Program — Boulder High School

  • April 22–26, 2019 proclaimed ISLA Appreciation Week
  • Program celebrates 25 years supporting first-generation Latin American students
  • 2018: 95%+ of 54 graduates enrolled in college; combined scholarships over $750,000

Permanent Supportive Housing — Lee Hill Road

  • Partnership: Boulder Housing Partners + Boulder Shelter for the Homeless
  • Opened November 2014; 31 units for chronically homeless individuals with at least one disability
  • Eligibility: HUD chronically homeless definition, Boulder resident, documented disability
  • Performance:
    • 99% occupancy rate in current year
    • Zero neighborhood complaints in four years of operation
    • Zero police calls regarding the facility
    • 12 residents have lived there since opening; 6 for 3+ years
  • High-utilizer resident outcomes:
    • One resident: 202 police contacts, 22 arrests, 200 nights in jail → zero police contacts since move-in
    • Another: 95 prior arrests → zero arrests since move-in
    • Five high-utilizer residents combined: 195 prior arrests, 3 police contacts, zero arrests since move-in
  • Goal achievement:
    • Housing retention at 2 years: 71% (86% for 2016–2018 cohort)
    • Improvement in income, life skills, employment: 97% success
    • Self-determination in health, substance abuse, education: 100% success
  • Neighborhood Advisory Committee meetings reduced to twice annually (neighbors requested reduction — no issues)

Family Homelessness

  • Documentary "Keep Families Housed" screened
  • 1,600+ Boulder families experiencing housing instability
  • 2,600 children in free/reduced lunch program; 1,000+ children living in poverty

Keep Families Housed — Rental Assistance Pilot

  • Two-year pilot: up to 3 payments of $500/family annually
  • Served 377 families (expanded from original 300-family goal)
  • Independent evaluation (Omni Institute) results:
    • Zero families became homeless during the year
    • Difficulty paying rent: 70% → 43%
    • Food insecurity: 63% → 40%
    • Skipping meals to feed children: 23% → 10%

Census 2020

  • City establishing Local Complete Count Committee
  • Key contacts: Julie Reeder (US Census Bureau Partnership Specialist), Lily Create Go (State Coordinator), Ryan Hansen (City Engagement Specialist — appointed to State Complete Count Committee)

Outcomes and Follow-Up

  1. ISLA Week proclaimed
  2. Permanent supportive housing report accepted; neighborhood meetings reduced to biannual
  3. Keep Families Housed to receive expanded funding from Colorado Health Foundation; Omni Institute to continue long-term evaluation
  4. Boulder County Connect Data System access beginning July 1 to improve tracking of repeat participants
  5. Family homelessness subcommittee (city, county, nonprofits) to complete integrated system design recommendations in ~6 months
  6. Census 2020: council to provide direction on Local Complete Count Committee composition
  7. Amended agenda approved to address University Hill Hotel and open space products items
