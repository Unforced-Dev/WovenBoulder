Date: March 24, 2020 Type: Special Meeting

Meeting Overview

Special meeting during the early COVID-19 surge to coordinate emergency response with Boulder County Public Health and align city and county stay-at-home orders. Boulder County reported 46 confirmed cases with modeling suggesting actual case counts may be 50× higher. The council voted to conform the city's emergency order to the county's forthcoming order.

Key Items

COVID-19 Case Statistics (as of 9:30 AM, March 24)

  • Boulder County: 46 confirmed cases
  • Colorado: 912 confirmed cases out of 7,701 tested (~11% positive rate)
  • 84 hospitalized statewide (~9% of positives)
  • ~70% of hospitalized individuals under age 65

Hospital Capacity

  • 168 staffed beds, 92 acute care beds, 12 ICU beds across county hospitals
  • Average hospitalization: ~3 weeks general; 10–14 days ICU plus additional floor time
  • Peak surge projected: late March through end of May depending on social mitigation effectiveness

Testing Constraints

  • Capacity insufficient; estimated actual cases 50× identified positives

County Health Order

  • Boulder County Health to issue emergency order within 24–48 hours
  • City committed to align order completely with county's language

Enforcement

  • Education and encouragement focused
  • Police will not arrest unless violations are repeated

Communications

  • Joint Information Center established to coordinate messaging
  • Commitment to provide information in Spanish and other languages

Outcomes and Follow-Up

  1. Council approved amended agenda 8-0
  2. Boulder County Health to issue emergency declaration within 24–48 hours
  3. City to amend emergency order to conform with county order by following day
  4. County and city implementing consistent "stay home" language regionally
  5. Emergency information to be provided in Spanish and other languages beyond English
  6. Hospital Association to prepare surge/alternative care facilities on standby
  7. State-level infection control taskforce to generate PPE supply
  8. Weekly monitoring with hospitals on surge preparation to continue
