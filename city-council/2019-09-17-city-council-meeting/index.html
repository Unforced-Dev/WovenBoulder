Date: September 17, 2019 Type: Regular Meeting

Meeting Overview

Regular meeting addressing the Community Court Initiative, tobacco Ordinance 3-40 second reading, immigration enforcement concerns about GEO Group/VI Incorporated in Boulder, and Boulder Creek's long-standing E. coli impairment.

Key Items

Community Court Initiative

  • Technical assistance grant from Center for Court Innovation
  • Site visits: three NYC courts and Spokane, WA
  • Spokane preferred model — similar university-town/homeless population profile
  • 2020 launch planned, avoiding September start
  • Warm handoffs between services already occurring informally
  • Key gap identified: no community engagement model currently in place
  • Judge Mary Logan from Spokane participated in Boulder planning discussions

Tobacco Ordinance 3-40

  • 40,000 deaths per year attributed to tobacco nationally
  • Colorado has highest high school vaping rates in the nation
  • Mint/menthol flavored products: 51% → 64% of youth use (increasing)
  • Trump administration announced removal of flavored vaping products from market
  • Michigan: first state to issue emergency rule banning flavored vaping
  • Federal action anticipated to align with local/state efforts
  • Council advancing ordinance focused on electronic smoking devices

GEO Group / VI Incorporated — Immigration Detention

  • GEO Group and VI Incorporated operate 71% of immigration detention centers nationwide
  • ISAP (Intensive Supervision Appearance Program) has presence in Boulder
  • Advocates reported being banned from check-in appointments
  • Ankle bracelets used as supervision mechanism
  • Community concerns raised about immigration enforcement infrastructure in Boulder

Boulder Creek E. Coli Impairment

  • Boulder Creek classified as E. coli impaired since 2003
  • No measurable improvement in the 16+ years since classification
  • E. coli Implementation Plan released June 2019

Outcomes and Follow-Up

  1. Community Court Initiative: 2020 launch target; staff to develop community engagement component
  2. Tobacco Ordinance 3-40 advancing; staff to monitor federal action on flavored vaping
  3. Staff to investigate GEO Group/VI Incorporated operations and advocate access issues
  4. Staff to report on E. coli Implementation Plan progress and Boulder Creek remediation timeline
