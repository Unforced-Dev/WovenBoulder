Date: July 16, 2019 Type: Regular Meeting

Meeting Overview

Regular meeting including Open Space and Mountain Parks accomplishments, a zero-waste community presentation, public comment on 5G health concerns ahead of the July 23 study session, affordable housing advocacy for the preservation of Mark House, and discussion of tobacco/nicotine regulation targeting youth. Agenda amendments included moving the climate emergency resolution and rescheduling the Hogan Pancos designation.

Key Items

OSMP Accomplishments

  • 1.9-mile trail completed providing access to Joan Or Ranch north of Boulder
  • North Trail Study Area Plan developed
  • 2013 flood recovery: rebuilt Neatest Valley Trail, new bridge over South Boulder Creek, Gregory Canyon Access Road repairs; 100+ flood recovery projects
  • 20,000+ volunteer hours; 630+ guided hikes reaching 15,000+ people
  • 146 acres of forest restored; prescribed fire conducted to reduce wildfire risk
  • Raptor facilitation: Peregrine Falcons, Golden Eagles, Bald Eagles; Raptor Monitoring Program 30th anniversary
  • 12 acres converted for agricultural/food production

Zero Waste

  • 90% of waste is recyclable or compostable
  • Recyclable: plastic bottles, tubs, jugs, jars; NOT plastic bags/wrap or coffee cups/lids
  • Compostable: all food waste including bones; NOT plastic-lined "compostable" items
  • Landfill: chip bags, squeeze tubes, styrofoam, candy wrappers

5G Technology

  • Study session scheduled July 23, 2019
  • Dr. Jonathan Sam (Dean, Colorado School of Health) allocated 15 minutes
  • Citizens requested addition of Dr. Devra Davis (Environmental Health Trust founder, former senior advisor to U.S. Asst. Secretary for Health) on RF radiation impacts on brain development, reproduction, and cancer
  • Previous Verizon 10-year cell lease at South Boulder Rec Center approved over citizen health objections

Mark House — Affordable Housing Preservation

  • 1128 Central Street: under contract with developer John Kirk
  • Proposed project: 16 three-bedroom luxury student housing apartments (replacing group residence serving 40 residents)
  • R-1 zoning; Kirk's prior documented violations: third-degree assault, underage sales, nuisance parties, criminal mischief; served jail time in Boulder for contempt of court
  • Mark House has served teachers, nurses, painters, carpenters for 46 years
  • Local nonprofit formed with donations/loans to purchase and preserve current use; 501(c)(3) with competitive offer

Tobacco and Nicotine Regulation

  • Boulder vaping/tobacco rates exceed national and state averages
  • U.S. Surgeon General declared vaping an epidemic; 18+ youth addicted for every adult user
  • Colorado communities implementing bans: Aspen, Basalt, Edgewater, Leadville, Snowmass Village
  • Current enforcement: ~$50 fines; teens using proxy purchasing systems
  • Request for ordinance: licensing, flavor bans, raising minimum age to 21

Dark Sky Ordinance

  • International Dark Sky Association member offered assistance with amendments
  • Current ordinance excludes streetlights and sports lighting — revision needed

Outcomes and Follow-Up

  1. Amended agenda approved including climate emergency resolution moved earlier
  2. 5G study session confirmed July 23; council to consider Dr. Devra Davis as additional presenter
  3. Mark House: referred to planning committee; nonprofit offer and developer interest under evaluation
  4. Out-of-session meeting to research city development fees distribution, departmental spending, and consultant expenditures
  5. Tobacco/nicotine enforcement and regulatory proposals deferred to later agenda items
