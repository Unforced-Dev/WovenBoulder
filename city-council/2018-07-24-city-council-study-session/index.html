Meeting Overview

Study session covering two major items: approval of an Intergovernmental Agreement (IGA) creating the Boulder County Regional Housing Partnership, and review of five focus areas for the Open Space and Mountain Parks Master Plan. The housing partnership aims to increase affordable housing from 10% to 12% of the county's housing stock through regional coordination. Transcript cuts off mid-discussion during the OSMP Master Plan focus area item; the vote on focus areas was not captured.

Key Items

Boulder County Regional Housing Partnership IGA — Unanimous Approval

  • New IGA creates the Boulder County Regional Housing Partnership
  • Current participants: City of Boulder, City of Longmont, and Boulder County
  • Goal: increase affordable housing from current 10% to 12% of county housing stock
  • Boulder contributes staffing resources (Kristen Highs' position as Partnership Manager) plus additional staff support
  • Other municipalities contribute HOME program administrative dollars; Boulder-Broomfield HOME Consortium received approximately $160,000 in federal HOME funds 2017–2018
  • IGA can be amended by steering committee approval; adding new jurisdictions requires administrative action
  • Partnership Champions (elected officials from each jurisdiction) to be seated by fall 2018
  • Regional Housing Strategy adopted by nine city councils across the county with five identified strategies
  • Vote: Unanimous approval

Open Space and Mountain Parks Master Plan — Five Focus Areas

  • Community engagement: 2,000 people engaged in first window (1,500 comments); 450 online responses in second window
  • Five focus areas proposed (Process Committee and Board of Trustees approved; Council approval requested):
    1. Ecosystem Health and Resilience — best available science; climate change preparation; carbon sequestration partnerships
    2. Responsible Recreational Enjoyment — enjoyment of nature balanced with obligation to protect it
    3. Agriculture — working landscapes in harmony with nature; integrates agricultural resources management plan
    4. Community Connection, Education and Inclusion — building a community of stewards
    5. Financial Sustainability — planning for future as open space system matures
  • Final Master Plan targeted for approval by end of summer 2019
  • Next steps: community workshops fall 2018 (October, November, December); statistically valid survey early 2019 for priorities and trade-offs
  • Separate "Community Engagement Framework" project underway for interpretive facilities and visitor centers
  • Council discussion: open space tax option would be included in financial sustainability discussions; website accessibility for newcomers flagged
  • Public comment: David Faris (reading Rebecca Trusting's remarks) advocated for education program expansion and renovation of Cherry Vale site as education center
  • Vote: Not recorded in transcript (cuts off before vote)

Outcomes and Follow-Up

  1. Boulder County Regional Housing Partnership officially established; commitment from Boulder, Longmont, and Boulder County; $160K in HOME funds committed 2017–2018
  2. Partnership Champions from each jurisdiction to be seated by fall 2018
  3. Five OSMP Master Plan focus areas confirmed pending final vote documentation
  4. Community workshops scheduled fall 2018 (October, November, December)
  5. Final master plan approval targeted for end of summer 2019
  6. Statistically valid survey to be conducted early 2019
  7. Community Engagement Framework project underway for interpretive facilities
  8. Cherry Vale site education center proposal to be considered by staff
