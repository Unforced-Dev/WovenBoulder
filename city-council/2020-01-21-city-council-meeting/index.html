Date: January 21, 2020 Type: Regular Meeting

Meeting Overview

Regular meeting with second readings of ordinances on building codes and tobacco regulation, plus extensive public comment on dark sky ordinance enforcement, housing occupancy policy, municipal utility finances, Chautauqua Association bylaws, and a historic landmark nomination. Board and commission recruitment was also announced.

Key Items

Ordinances — Second Readings

  • Ordinance 83-66: Amended international building codes
  • Ordinance 83-76: Tobacco sales and regulation

Boards and Commissions Recruitment

  • Applications open through February 14 at 5 pm
  • City residency requirement only (no citizenship requirement)

Dark Sky Ordinance Enforcement

  • Concerns about selective enforcement in Boulder Meadows Mobile Home Park
  • Residential outdoor lighting not regulated despite ordinance requirements

Housing Occupancy Ordinance

  • Concerns about Ordinance 9-8 Section 5 limiting unrelated occupants
  • Alleged discriminatory impact on lower-income and non-traditional households

Municipal Utility (Muni)

  • $23 million in costs over 8 years ($2–3/resident/month)
  • Concerns about transparency in financial analysis

Chautauqua Association Bylaws

  • Colorado Chautauqua Association seeking governance modernization
  • Bylaws update approved by board 12-2; requires 75% member vote for adoption
  • Concern: vote-buying through bulk membership purchases
  • City attorney raised potential lease breach concerns

Mott House Historic Landmark

  • Designation proposal for Twelfth Street property
  • Unanimous landmarks department recommendation for preservation

Outcomes and Follow-Up

  1. Staff to investigate dark sky ordinance enforcement and selective enforcement concerns in mobile home parks
  2. Two-minute public comment limit confirmed; three minutes only when two or fewer speakers
  3. Chautauqua Association proceeding with 75% member approval threshold for bylaws amendment
  4. Mott House historic landmark designation advancing to council for final approval
  5. Board and commission applications remain open through February 14 at 5 pm
