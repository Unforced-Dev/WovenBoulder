Date: May 5, 2020 Type: Regular Meeting

Meeting Overview

Regular meeting covering COVID-19 case trends, transition from “Stay at Home” to “Safer at Home” (effective May 6/8), and a face covering order effective May 8–26. Boulder Community Health (Trinity Health) reported only 3 current COVID-19 inpatients with 10% ventilator usage versus 25% nationally, suggesting favorable local outcomes. Nearly 400 businesses participated in social distancing guideline development.

Key Items

COVID-19 Case Data

  • Boulder County: 668 total cases
  • Age distribution: 20–29 and 50–59 highest case concentration; 60–80+ most severe outcomes
  • Community-wide spread ongoing; travel-related transmission essentially eliminated
  • Long-term care facilities contain majority of confirmed cases

Public Health Orders

  • “Stay at Home” expires May 7; “Safer at Home” begins May 8
  • “Safer at Home”: ~60–65% social distancing effectiveness (vs. 75% under prior order)
  • Face Covering Order: effective May 8 through May 26, 2020
  • Cloth coverings recommended; N95/surgical masks reserved for healthcare workers

Testing Capacity

  • ~4,500 cumulative tests in Boulder County; ~100/day current rate
  • Target: 500 comprehensive tests/day within one month
  • Infection Prevention & Control staff increased from 17 to 26
  • Worst-case staffing need: 37 staff for 50 cases/day

Hospital Data (Trinity Health / Boulder Community Health)

  • Total COVID-19 patients treated: 43
  • Currently hospitalized: 3 (down from 20+ two weeks prior)
  • Deaths: 7 (majority from senior living facilities)
  • Discharged: 33 (83%)
  • Average length of stay: 30 days
  • Ventilator use: 10% of admitted patients (vs. 25% nationally)
  • Mortality rate: significantly below national average
  • Employee testing: 150 employees tested; 10% positive (majority community-acquired, early in pandemic)
  • Elective surgeries resumed successfully

Business Engagement

  • ~400 businesses participated in social distancing guideline development
  • John Tear (Boulder City Chamber) and Entering Waldo (Chamber) coordinated effort

Mortality Context

  • Boulder County mortality rate among lowest compared to surrounding counties including Douglas County and Denver metro

Outcomes and Follow-Up

  1. “Safer at Home” transition effective May 8
  2. Face Covering Order in effect May 8–26; emphasis on social distancing first, masks when 6-foot distance not possible
  3. Testing expansion toward 500/day target within one month
  4. Staffing scale-up from 26 toward 37 FTE capacity for higher case loads
  5. Long-term care facility support and isolation planning coordination ongoing
  6. State team testing asymptomatic residents at 60% of Boulder facilities without confirmed cases
  7. Targeted cultural community outreach for testing access
  8. Resume public messaging: hospitals safe for non-COVID emergencies; do not delay care for stroke or cardiac events
