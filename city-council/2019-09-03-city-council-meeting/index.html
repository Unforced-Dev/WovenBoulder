Meeting Overview

Regular meeting with proclamations for Pollinators Appreciation Month, LGBTQ+ Pride Week, and a day honoring Dr. Charles Cambridge and Elaine Takahashi for pioneering Educational Opportunity Programs at CU. Significant public comment on zoning/density policy ahead of an October 1 vote, and discussion of the Open Space Master Plan and proposed 20-year tax.

LGBTQ+ Pride Week (September 3–8)

  • OutBoulder County: 20+ years serving the community
  • Police liaison arm established as part of community relationship building
  • Stonewall 50th anniversary: dance/Riot commemorations

Dr. Charles Cambridge and Elaine Takahashi Day — September 14

  • First American Indian and Asian American Educational Opportunity Programs at CU Boulder, founded 1969
  • 112 American Indian students enrolled by fall 1971
  • First Asian American history course offered 1970
  • Programs expanded to CU Denver 1972
  • 60+ alumni reunion held in conjunction with the proclamation

Open Space Master Plan and 20-Year Tax

  • Open Space Master Plan and proposed 20-year sales tax extension discussed
  • Community input process ongoing
