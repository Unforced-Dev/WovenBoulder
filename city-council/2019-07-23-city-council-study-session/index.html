5G Technology Panel (60 minutes)

How 5G Works

  • Three frequency bands: low-band (<1 GHz), mid-band (1–6 GHz), high-band/millimeter wave (24–100 GHz)
  • Low/mid-band 5G: same power levels as existing WiFi and 4G LTE used for 30+ years
  • High-band/millimeter wave: ~100m coverage per antenna (vs. 4–5 mile diameter for traditional cell towers); requires significantly denser infrastructure

Regulatory Framework

  • Federal Telecom Act (1996) governs local government authority
  • FCC September 2018 order: materiality test for "effectively prohibit" service standards
  • Colorado 2017 small cell bill: authorized use by right in any zoning district; allows batch permit applications

5G Use Cases

  • Commercial mobile broadband in high-density urban areas
  • Industrial IoT and automation; augmented reality; smart city applications
  • Enhanced mobile broadband (latency as low as 1 millisecond vs. 4G)
  • Remote surgery (theoretical)

Standards Timeline

  • 3GPP Release 15 (H1 2018): Non-standalone 5G leveraging existing LTE
  • 3GPP Release 16 (H2 2018): Standalone 5G for greenfield deployments
  • Products typically appear 18–24 months after standards completion
