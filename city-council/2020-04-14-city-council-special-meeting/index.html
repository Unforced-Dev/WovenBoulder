Meeting Overview

Special meeting focused on COVID-19 epidemiological briefing from Boulder County Public Health. Officials reviewed case trends, hospital capacity, testing supply shortages, and the Governor's framework for lifting stay-at-home orders. Council asked about mask mandates, first responder housing, and contact tracing capacity.

Key Items

COVID-19 Case Data

  • Boulder County: 200+ confirmed cases
  • Age distribution: highest concentration in 20–29 year-olds; 60–80+ showing most serious outcomes
  • 40–45% of cases estimated asymptomatic

Hospital Capacity

  • Medical/surge bed availability: high and stable
  • ICU bed availability: increasing (positive trend)
  • Ventilator usage: below 50% capacity
  • Regional surge facilities anticipated within 10–14 days

Governor's Reopening Criteria (American Enterprise Institute framework)

  • 14-day sustained reduction in case counts required
  • Hospital capacity to treat patients without crisis standards
  • Expanded testing capability
  • Sufficient contact tracing capacity
  • Decision on April 26 order pending

Testing Constraints

  • Significant shortages of testing swabs and PPE
  • State unable to meet full testing demand
  • Electronic symptom screening mechanism anticipated within 2 weeks
  • 15 contact tracers being trained; additional state-level staffing underway

Mask Guidance

  • Voluntary recommendation from Governor and public health
  • Cloth masks preferred; N95/surgical masks reserved for healthcare workers
  • Colorado Mask Project website referenced for cloth mask resources
  • Enforcement challenges discussed

First Responder Housing

  • Hotel accommodations website for first responders concerned about family exposure referenced
  • Follow-up with Interim Housing Director Frank Alexander committed

Outcomes and Follow-Up

  1. Governor decision on April 26 stay-at-home order pending; 14-day sustained reduction required to lift
  2. County Health to follow up with Frank Alexander on first responder housing options
  3. State electronic symptom screening/testing referral system to launch within 2 weeks
  4. 15 contact tracers in training; state and local agencies scaling up
  5. Regional surge facility setup continuing; hospitals coordinating mutual support
  6. State PPE and testing supply chain task force continuing alternate sourcing efforts
