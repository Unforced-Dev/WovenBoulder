Meeting Overview

Regular meeting honoring Nancy Dyer's retirement after 18 years as director of the Boulder History Museum, including her role creating the Museum of Boulder (40,000 visitors since opening). The meeting also addressed police accountability following a confrontation with disabled Black resident Sammy Lawrence on April 5, a new legal aid program for low-income residents, North Boulder Library expansion, and pedestrian transportation planning.

Key Items

Nancy Dyer — Museum of Boulder

  • 18 years leading Boulder History Museum; retiring
  • Created the Museum of Boulder at the former Masonic Lodge (Broadway and Pine)
  • Fundraising: nearly $10M raised, including $4M from 2014 voter-approved city tax initiative
  • Nearly 40,000 visitors in less than one year since opening
  • Features: Google Garage Maker Space, 100+ seat auditorium, rooftop deck, planned Children's Discovery Zone (ages 0–8)
  • Collections: tens of thousands of artifacts, 1M+ documents and photos
  • Exhibits: Boulder Nobel Laureates, Olympians, NIST atomic clock, Ball Aerospace Kepler satellite model

Police Accountability — Sammy Lawrence Incident

  • April 5, 2019: Officers demanded Lawrence (disabled, Black resident) drop his walking staff (mobility aid) at Mapleton Ball Fields
  • Officers assaulted him, removed the mobility aid, and arrested him
  • Charges: resisting arrest and obstruction of justice; walking aid returned broken after several days
  • Public demands: independent external investigation, officers removed from duty pending investigation
  • Context cited: Black Americans twice as likely to be killed by police; disabled individuals account for 27–81% of police-killed individuals
  • Contrast: Aaron White (white, able-bodied) filmed officers same day at same location — received cordial treatment

Bridge to Justice Legal Services

  • Launched January 2019; located at 1123 Spruce St, Suite 200, Boulder
  • Funded by Boulder's Sugar Sweetened Beverage Distribution Tax
  • Free legal assistance for residents at or below 80% AMI: Medicaid, SNAP, health exchange plans, tax credits
  • Staff Attorney Troll Hays: 30+ years in benefits law
  • General legal services at ~$115/hr (one-third market rate); healthcare/food assistance project fully grant-funded

North Boulder Library

  • Project moving forward; proposed full branch to serve Boulder Meadows and Ponderosa Mobile Home Parks, PHP communities, Holiday neighborhood (40% affordable units)
  • Features: dedicated children's programming space, larger collection, community meeting rooms

Prairie Dog Management

  • City adopted no-kill policy ~10 years prior; prairie dogs expanded to neighboring private farmland
  • Result: 1,000+ acres of irrigated farmland destroyed; ~20,000 prairie dogs relocated or killed by neighbors
  • Public testimony urging lethal control program on irrigated farmland

Pedestrian Transportation

  • Pedestrian Advisory Committee informing Pedestrian Plan and Transportation Master Plan
  • Identified challenges: fast traffic on Canyon Blvd, insufficient crosswalks, gaps in pedestrian infrastructure
  • Recommendations: allocate transportation budget to walking/biking; repurpose asphalt for pedestrians; Vision Zero + SOV reduction; model after Pearl Street Mall

Outcomes and Follow-Up

  1. Nancy Dyer honored with formal declaration
  2. Police accountability: independent external investigation demanded; council to consider removing officers pending investigation
  3. Bridge to Justice to receive referrals from City Council; potential additional grant funding discussed
  4. North Boulder Library expansion confirmed to move forward
  5. Transportation budget to be directed toward pedestrian and biking infrastructure
  6. Prairie dog management policy: transition toward lethal control on irrigated farmland under consideration
  7. Seven board/commission positions open for recruitment
