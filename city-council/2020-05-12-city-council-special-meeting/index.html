Meeting Overview

Special meeting for COVID-19 briefing covering case trends, testing expansion strategy, and hospital capacity. Boulder County reported 775 total cases with a 2% positivity rate — dramatically lower than statewide and national rates of 20–30%. Council also discussed camera/recording protocols for future virtual meetings.

Key Items

COVID-19 Case Data

  • Boulder County: 775 cases
  • 5-day average of new cases: declining (positive trend)
  • Positivity rate: ~2% (vs. 20–30% statewide and nationally)
  • Long-term care facilities account for majority of deaths

Hospital Capacity (Boulder Community Health)

  • Total COVID-19 patients treated through pandemic: 44
  • Last week: 4 patients hospitalized
  • ICU: typically 2–4 patients; significant reserve capacity
  • Surge bed capacity: adequate across county hospitals

Testing Strategy

  • Primary focus: expand PCR testing
  • Community testing sites being established with local healthcare providers
  • Serological (antibody) testing: 12 FDA-approved tests available; 100–200 more in pipeline
  • Priority populations: essential workers, adults 65+, chronic conditions, Latino communities, first responders, long-term care facilities, skilled nursing/assisted living

Contact Tracing

  • State identified 1,000 contact tracers through AmeriCorps
  • Large gatherings cited as contact tracing challenge (example: restaurant in Wright County, MN)

Key Personnel

  • Jeff Zayak — Public Health Director presenting disease trends and testing roadmap
  • Dr. Chris — presenting community testing strategy
  • Dr. K. Moon — Chief Medical Officer, Boulder Community Health, presenting hospital status

Public Health Guidance

  • 6-foot social distancing as primary mechanism
  • Masks recommended when 6 feet cannot be maintained
  • “Safer at Home” order recently in effect (began May 8)

Outcomes and Follow-Up

  1. PCR testing expansion to continue; phased approach prioritizing vulnerable populations and essential workers
  2. Multiple community testing sites to be established county-wide
  3. Serological testing evaluation underway to inform future diagnostic protocols
  4. Weekly hospital coordination meetings continuing to monitor surge capacity and ICU availability
  5. AmeriCorps contact tracers deployed; ongoing monitoring of transmission sources and disparity impacts
  6. Social distancing and mask compliance assessment planned for following 1–2 weeks
  7. Council to discuss and establish camera protocols for virtual meetings
