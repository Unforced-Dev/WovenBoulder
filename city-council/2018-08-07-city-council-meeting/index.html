Meeting Overview

Regular meeting coinciding with National Night Out, prompting the council to skip open public comment. Featured a special declaration recognizing the Emergency Family Assistance Association's 100th anniversary, board and commission vacancy appointments process, and discussion of a significant rezoning proposal for the Mapleton area. Key Items

Declaration — EFAA 100th Anniversary (August 11 = EFAA Day)

  • EFAA established in 1918 during the Spanish flu pandemic when Boulder had 10,000 residents
  • Community safety net for families with children, seniors, and people with disabilities for 100 years
  • During the Great Depression, 25% of Boulder households needed EFAA assistance
  • Operates on 80% private funding, almost entirely local; no federal funding
  • Services: food, financial assistance, housing, case management, family resource services
  • Now operates Boulder's Family Resource Center
  • EFAA Day celebration: Saturday, August 11, 10 a.m.–2 p.m. at farmers market
  • Council members received EFAA branded t-shirts; declaration adopted

Board and Commission Appointments Process

  • Four board vacancies to be filled
  • Subcommittee (Mary and Lisa) to present applicant packets to full council August 14
  • Housing Advisory Board requires expanded criteria; will be reopened for new applications with 10-day window
  • Interviews to be conducted for Housing Advisory Board applicants only
  • Timeline: August 14 (study session/discussion), August 21 (special meeting with all votes)

Diversity and Inclusion Update (Removed)

  • Originally Item One B; moved off August 7 agenda
  • Rescheduled to August 14 regular meeting to allow public comment
  • Motion to move passed unanimously

311 Mapleton Rezoning — Second Reading (Deferred)

  • Property owner signed covenant as discussed in previous council session
  • Covenant conditions precedent: entitlements for 1655 33rd Street by end of year; development review approvals for 311 Mapleton finalized; development agreement; tax credit approval
  • Tax credit clarification: Covenant to be amended to specify 4% tax credits (easier to secure) rather than 9% (highly competitive)
  • Units under 450 sq ft: affordability requirements set by City rather than CHFA
  • Design Advisory Board feedback requested on form, bulk, and massing; Jim Robertson to solicit input during permitting and tech talks
  • Vote deferred pending design review and covenant refinement

Consent Agenda Items

  • Charles Nylon Scholarship: City contribution approved to CU scholarship honoring Professor Nylon, who founded HELP in 1968 following MLK's assassination; HELP created affordable housing for families of color and elderly residents on fixed incomes (1968–1977); scholarship designated for education students pursuing teacher licensure committed to underresourced schools
  • Resolution Opposing Initiative 108: Industry-funded initiative that would curtail state/local governments' ability to regulate if actions reduce private property market value; would affect land use, zoning, oil and gas protections, and health and safety regulations; resolution adopted

Outcomes and Follow-Up

  1. August 11, 2018 officially designated as EFAA Day; community celebration 10 a.m.–2 p.m. at farmers market
  2. Board appointment votes all scheduled for August 21 special meeting
  3. Housing Advisory Board: 10-day application window with new criteria; interviews to follow
  4. 311 Mapleton: covenant language refinement ongoing; Design Advisory Board review required; 4% tax credit financing confirmed; vote deferred
  5. Diversity and Inclusion Update rescheduled to August 14 for full public comment
  6. Initiative 108 opposition resolution adopted; council to encourage public awareness
