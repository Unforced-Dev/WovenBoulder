AI Summary

A shorter virtual meeting with commissioners JH, Carlos, Anna, and one other (Christine absent). Staff: Ingrid (facilitating), Elizabeth (HHS). Guest: Julia Daly, new Human Investments Manager overseeing the Human Relations Fund. The meeting covered sign-ups for the Independent Police Monitor interview panel, a racial equity training debrief, staff updates on the CARE co-responder program and police oversight ordinance, and commissioner updates on the Human Relations Fund, Boulder Junction Phase 2, and community block parties. JH declined to participate in the Police Monitor interview panel, citing two ongoing cases against Boulder PD stemming from a February 2023 incident at his business.

Key Topics

Independent Police Monitor Interview Panel City conducting a second round of interviews for the Independent Police Monitor position (first round yielded no hire). HRC members invited to join the interview panel on July 11-12. JH declined: he has two ongoing cases against Boulder PD following a February 2023 incident where officers came to his business claiming he stole things — still no police report two weeks out. Anna was interested but uncertain about schedule. Carlos agreed to participate; commission gave consensus approval. Ingrid to send Carlos details.

Racial Equity Training: "Advancing Racial Equity — The Role of Government" Anna and another commissioner attended the same session and found it valuable. Highlights: interactive surveys with real-time bar graphs showing diverse responses, small-group discussions that built trust and allowed deeper conversation on uncomfortable topics. Carlos's Spanish-language session is upcoming. Christine will attend a future session. All commissioners encouraged to complete it.

CARE Program / Reimagining Police (Staff Update) Clinician and case manager positions posted and hiring underway. Paramedic position opening soon; Fire Department working on acquiring a van for the response team. City awaiting evaluation vendor proposals (selection expected that week). Soft launch targeted for end of July 2023; full launch early September 2023. Program pairs mental health/fire personnel (no police) with non-crime 9-1-1 calls.

Police Oversight Ordinance Community Conversation Community meeting scheduled for June 21, 2023 (day after this meeting), 5-6:45 PM, Canyon Theatre at Boulder Public Library. Spanish interpretation provided. Written feedback link shared in chat for those unable to attend in person. Commissioners invited to share the link with community members.

Administrative Reorganization (Staff Note) Ongoing conversations with city manager's office about relocating the HRC administratively to a different city office. No updates; process continuing.

Human Relations Fund — Round 1 Update (Julia Daly) Julia Daly introduced as new Human Investments Manager and HRF manager. Round 1 applications open through September 29, 2023 for events June–December. Of $47,000 total budget, $42,250 already allocated; only $4,750 remaining (earmarked for Indigenous People's Day). Awards funded Immigrant Heritage Month, Juneteenth, Pride, Indigenous People's Day events. Application process described as streamlined and accessible.

Boulder Junction Phase 2 (Anna) Anna attending a multi-board working group on Boulder Junction, a 15-year mixed-use development project east of downtown (near 30th/Folsom and Pearl/Baseline, close to Google). Phase 1 complete (mixed use). Phase 2: ~1,400 new housing units, ~2,800 potential jobs, expanded green/public spaces, trail connections, small business spaces. Next multi-board meeting in July; Planning Board public hearing in August; City Council public hearing in September.

Community Block Parties (Carlos) Carlos sharing update on city-sponsored block parties at manufactured home communities. Upcoming: June 24, 1-5 PM at Orchard Grove (30th and Baseline). Future events planned at Boulder Meadows, Village on Mapleton, and possibly a central location. Calendar to be shared with commission. Collaboration between engagement team, HHS, and other city departments.

Letter to Council on Homelessness Commissioner Speaker and Christine drafting a letter to City Council; no update presented as Christine was absent and Speaker was attending by phone while driving.
