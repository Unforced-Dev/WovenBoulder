Springs Valley Estates Annexation

In Progress 1 meeting

Status: In Progress — Planning Board approved 4-0; City Council second hearing May 21, 2026 Lead Body: City Council Related Bodies: Planning Board, Boulder Fire Rescue First Active: 2023 (petition filed) Case Number: LUR2023-00001

Springs Valley Estates is a 36-home North Boulder subdivision (Linden Drive / Cholla Court / Spring Valley Road / Cactus Court) developed between 1966–1977 that is seeking annexation into the City of Boulder. The primary driver is fire safety: the Springs Valley Mutual Water Association (SVMWA) private wells cannot sustain fire suppression pressure. The neighborhood was evacuated during the Goat Trail Fire approximately two weeks before the April 2026 Planning Board hearing.

The annexation enables connection to Boulder's public water system and qualifies the district for a $1 million DOLA (Department of Local Affairs) grant — contingent on annexation being completed by November 14, 2026. The annexation is proceeding by election because not all property owners signed the petition.

  • LID (Local Improvement District) cost estimates to be converted from "preliminary" to "final" per board direction
