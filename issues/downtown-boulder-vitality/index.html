Downtown Boulder Vitality

Ongoing 6 meetings

Status: Ongoing Lead Body: Downtown Management Commission Related Bodies: City Council, Planning Board, Boulder Urban Renewal Authority Active Since: Ongoing (perennial)

Downtown Boulder — centered on the Pearl Street Mall and surrounding blocks — is managed through the Downtown Boulder Business Improvement District (BID), overseen by the Downtown Management Commission (DMC). The DMC manages programming, events, marketing, and capital improvements to support a vibrant retail and commercial district.

Challenges include post-pandemic retail recovery, changing consumer behavior, managing the pedestrian experience on Pearl Street, and balancing the interests of property owners, businesses, and visitors. The DMC coordinates with the city on urban renewal and streetscape improvements. BURA manages tax increment financing that can support downtown revitalization.

The most significant near-term development is the city's exploration of a Downtown Development Authority (DDA) — a state-enabled tool combining property TIF, sales TIF, mill levy, and parking assets to fund downtown improvements. A November 2026 ballot measure is being planned.
