2952 Baseline Road Redevelopment

Land Use & Planning In Progress 3 meetings

Status: In Progress — Concept review feedback given; designation hearing scheduled Lead Body: Planning Board Domain: Land Use and Zoning · Historic Preservation Last Seen: 2026-05

Proposed redevelopment of a strip mall at 2952 Baseline Road into pedestrian-friendly mixed-use development, tied to the Coburn/Unity Church site. Raises historic preservation vs. affordable housing trade-off concerns, similar to the 990 Arapahoe situation. The Environmental Advisory Board also reviewed for environmental feedback.
